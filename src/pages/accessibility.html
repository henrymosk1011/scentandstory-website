Our commitment

Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed October 14, 2026

Scent and Story Perfume Bar wants every visitor to enjoy this website, including people who use assistive technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

What we have done

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a link to skip straight to the main content.
  • All animation respects your device’s reduce motion setting. When it is turned on, scrolling effects are switched off and the content is presented in a calm, static layout.
  • Images include descriptive text alternatives, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and interface colors meet WCAG contrast requirements, and the layout adapts to zoom levels up to 400 percent.
  • Headings, landmarks, and lists are structured so screen readers can navigate the page efficiently.
  • The inquiry form uses clear labels, explains which fields are required, and announces errors and confirmations to assistive technology.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is an ongoing commitment. We test the site with automated tools and by hand, and we review it whenever new content is added.

Need help or have feedback?

If anything on this site is difficult to use, please let us know. Email {{site.email}} and tell us the page and the problem you ran into. We will respond as quickly as we can and provide the information you need in another format if helpful.
