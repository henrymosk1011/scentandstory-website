The art of a blend

A fragrance unfolds in three acts

Like every good story, a perfume has an opening, a heart, and an ending that stays with you. Our mixologist helps each guest balance all three.

  1. Act I

    Top notes

    The first impression.

    Bright and fleeting, they greet you in the very first moments. Think sparkling citrus, crisp fruit, and fresh green herbs.

  2. Act II

    Heart notes

    The soul of the scent.

    As the opening fades, the heart blooms and gives a fragrance its character. Think rose, jasmine, peony, and soft spice.

  3. Act III

    Base notes

    The memory that lingers.

    Rich and slow to fade, the base holds the whole story together for hours. Think vanilla, musk, sandalwood, and warm woods.

The experience

From first note to final bottle

Five unhurried steps. One bottle that belongs to no one else.

  1. 01

    Arrive at the bar

    Guests are welcomed to a beautifully styled perfume bar set within your celebration, every bottle waiting to be discovered.

  2. 02

    Discover your notes

    Smell, compare, and fall for the notes that feel like you. Sweet and floral, woody and warm, fresh and bright.

  3. 03

    Blend with a mixologist

    A professional fragrance mixologist guides each guest through a personal blend, balancing every note with care.

  4. 04

    Bottle the moment

    Every creation is bottled in 15mL or 30mL and finished with an elegant label, ready to wear before the night is over.

  5. 05

    Leave wearing the memory

    Long after the last dance, a single spritz brings the whole evening back.
