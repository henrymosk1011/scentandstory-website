Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Scent and Story Perfume Bar (“Scent and Story,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through {{site.url}}, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you send an inquiry, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, event date, location, guest count, and any message you write.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. This is used to keep the site secure and running smoothly.

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy to describe them.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and plan your event.
  • To send proposals, confirmations, and other messages related to your booking.
  • To protect our website from spam, abuse, and security threats.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for cross context behavioral advertising.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to run this website: Cloudflare hosts the site and helps protect it, and Resend delivers inquiry form messages to our inbox. These providers process information only on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any resulting booking, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices and California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to request that we correct or delete it, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, email us at {{site.email}}. We will verify and respond to your request as required by law.

Our site does not respond differently to Do Not Track signals because we do not track visitors across other websites.

Children

This website is intended for adults planning events. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Our site is served entirely over an encrypted connection, and we take reasonable measures to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Photographs on this website

Every photograph shown on this website is owned by Scent and Story or is used with the permission of its owner. These photos are protected by copyright and may not be copied, downloaded for reuse, or republished without our written permission.

Many of our photos are taken at real celebrations. If you appear in a photo on this site and would like it removed, email us at {{site.email}} and we will take it down.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above.
